# Somewhere, Eventually
*A Travel Journal for the In-Between*

---

### Concept & Philosophy
**Somewhere, Eventually** is an independent cinematic travel publication and visual journal dedicated to places, atmosphere, and the liminal textures of transit. It rejects transactional booking widgets, algorithmic "top-ten" checklists, and curated vacation packages in favor of the contemplation that occurs while waiting on cold railway platforms, walking down unfamiliar alleys in an autumn drizzle, and observing places that exist for themselves.

---

### Key Editorial Features & Pages

1. **Home / Journal Front**
   - Headline and lead curatorial manifesto set out in the open with classical editorial proportions.
   - Featured territory spread with full-bleed environmental photography, field excerpts, and technical metadata.
   - Five Landscapes interactive portfolio with category filters and country notes.
   - Field notebook excerpts written by correspondents in transit.
   - Lead story feature callout.

2. **Destinations (Editorial Territories)**
   - Five complete editorial profiles with bespoke 3-image photo spreads (Wide Environmental Horizon, Intimate Tactile Detail, Environmental Portrait / Architecture):
     - **Reinefjorden & The Outer Skerries** (Lofoten, Norway — Coastal)
     - **The Furka Switchbacks** (Swiss Alps — Alpine Corridor)
     - **Yanaka & The Drizzling Lanes** (Tokyo, Japan — Urban Alleyways)
     - **The 17:42 at Torrenieri** (Tuscany, Italy — Rural Waypoint)
     - **Cape Sound & The Shingle Edge** (Dungeness, UK — Edge Lands)
   - Real-time category filtering and search.
   - Acoustic fieldscape documentation, coordinates, and vernacular field notes.

3. **Featured Travel Story (Longform Reader)**
   - *"On Waiting for the Night Train to Narvik"* by Elena Lindqvist.
   - Dynamic scroll-triggered reading progress bar.
   - Sticky chapter navigation directory with live chapter highlights.
   - Reading tone switcher: Linen Paper, Darkroom Basalt, and Sepia Archive.
   - Drop caps, narrative pull-quotes, ambient telemetry notes, and embedded archival film negatives.

4. **Colophon & Philosophy**
   - The four editorial tenets of observation.
   - Hardware and physical emulsion specifications (medium format, silver gelatin films, Munken Lynx paper stock).
   - Field correspondent profiles with camera equipment and dispatch notes.
   - Interactive Dispatch Submission form with real-time preview plate and session archive.

5. **Acoustic & Photographic Features**
   - Procedural Web Audio API soundscape synthesizer producing organic ambient transit audio (low frequency rail rumble and gentle coastal wash).
   - Full-screen Photographic Plate Inspector modal with EXIF camera details (camera body, optics, film stock, exposure settings, lighting, and geographic coordinates).

---

### Technical Stack & Quality Assurance
- **Framework:** React 19 + TypeScript + Vite
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS v4 + Custom Literary Typography System (Cormorant Garamond & Newsreader)
- **Animation:** Framer Motion (restrained spatial continuity & entrance transitions)
- **Audited with `design-taste-frontend`:** Zero FAIL measurements; fully compliant with anti-slop standards (no artificial hairlines, no arbitrary card outlines, no decorative index numbering, no covered text, and strict 4.5:1+ contrast).
